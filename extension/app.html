Comment Cleaner for 9GAG

Sign in in exactly the newly opened 9gag.com/login tab (not another tab or an Incognito window — those have a separate sign-in).

9GAG has no list of a user's own comments — the extension loads the posts you have commented on and walks through their comments and replies to find yours. Scanning starts with the first posts right away and covers several posts at once ("Posts in parallel"; more is faster but makes it more likely that 9GAG starts throttling requests). The page limit (50 top-level comments per page; reply threads are always read in full) caps the time spent on posts with thousands of comments.
